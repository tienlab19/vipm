## Review notes

PSPOPrep is a local-first exam-preparation app. No account is required.

Version 1.0.0 (build 5) includes the complete 800-question bank and all study modes for every user at no charge. In-App Purchase is disabled in this build: there is no paywall, purchase, restore, or offer-code screen, and no StoreKit product or transaction requests. No IAP product is included in this submission.

To review the study features:
1. Launch the app and complete or skip onboarding.
2. Open Practice to access any question-bank part, or Exam to start an 80-question, 60-minute mock exam.
3. Open Flash Challenge or Time Trial from Home. Save a question during practice, then open Saved to review it.
4. Incorrect and Missed review lists populate after answering incorrectly or submitting an attempt with unanswered questions.

The app requests notification permission only after the user saves a planned exam date. Notifications are local reminders. Study progress, learner name, planned exam date, bookmarks, answers, and drafts remain on device in UserDefaults and are not uploaded.

Firebase Analytics records app usage events without an app account or developer-supplied user ID. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use IDFA for cross-app tracking.

Question images, when present in the licensed question bank, load over HTTPS. Core practice content is bundled in the app.

The app is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with Scrum.org. Rights to distribute the bundled question bank must be confirmed before submission.
